Statement

It is almost a dream that existence can be felt at all, a fleeting apparition, permitted to humanity alone in its fullness, while other creatures remain confined to mere impulses, never conscious of even a fraction of what we endure.

There is no need to probe the meaning of life when we are simply being, whether in any character or temperament, in any physical or biological form, or under any ideology

With the singular vibration of every particle within us, briefly making the flow of space and time shimmer into appearance.

What strikes me with greatest force, making my body tremble like a tree in the late winds of autumn, is the vast disproportion between the immeasurable cosmic history and the vanishing instant of the present.

Its lucky that i cought some of that moment being of mine.
