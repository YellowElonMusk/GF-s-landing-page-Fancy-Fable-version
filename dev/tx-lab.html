Text

The reel changes and the letters go soft before they lift off as silver grain. Nothing in the column moves: the blocks that hold the words go transparent and the canvas paints them from then on, so underlines, chips and markers stay exactly where they are.

Grains drift in the beam while the next page is fetched, then leave on wandering arcs with long-exposure streaks. Most bow one way, a visible minority the other, and the heading comes up last on the beat the camera stops.

Under the fold nothing is ever hidden: only glyphs within half a viewport of the edge join the effect, so a long page keeps its captions while the top half burns. A link inside a paragraph fades its underline with its word.
